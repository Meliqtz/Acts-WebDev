The Prancing Horse

The black horse comes from Francesco Baracca, an Italian fighter pilot in World War I who painted it on his plane. After he died in 1918, his mother suggested Enzo Ferrari put the horse on his cars for good luck. Enzo added the yellow background, the color of his hometown, Modena.

Signature color
Rosso Corsa

"Racing red," Italy's traditional racing color

Enzo Ferrari

Born in 1898, Enzo was a racing driver before he became a team owner. He started Scuderia Ferrari in 1929 to race Alfa Romeo cars, and built the first car under his own name in 1947. He led the company until his death in 1988.
